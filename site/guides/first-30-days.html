Your first 30 days in Paraguay

Just arrived

Who this is for: people who just arrived in Paraguay, or who are packing for arrival next month.

What it covers: a first-month logistics checklist, phone basics, housing and transport habits, banking product basics, health pointers at a high level, and meeting people.

What it does not cover: residency categories, immigration document lists, fees, timelines, apostille rules, cédula steps, RUC registration, proof-of-funds rules, insurance rules for immigration, TIN acceptance by banks, or cross-border tax advice. Arrival checklist

Use this as a starter logistics list. Legal paperwork steps are not listed here.

First 48 hours

  • Confirm temporary lodging and how you will pay (card and cash backup).
  • Get a working local number or roaming plan so banks, hosts, and services can reach you. Licensed mobile operators named in CONATEL materials include TELECEL (Tigo), NÚCLEO (Personal), AMX (Claro), and Nubicom. An international eSIM can cover you until then (see section 3).
  • Keep digital and paper copies of the identity documents you already travel with.
  • Note the address you will use for deliveries and bank letters.

First two weeks

  • Open or prepare a local bank relationship for guaraní bills when you qualify (see banking section).
  • Learn a few everyday Spanish phrases for taxis, shops, and clinics.
  • Introduce yourself in New arrivals inside the free ParaguayNext community.
  • Details on immigration appointments, local ID, and tax registration are coming later. For now, check with a licensed professional in Paraguay.

First 30 days

  • Stabilize housing (short extension or a careful longer lease).
  • Set up a layered money setup (international ATM card + local bank + backup card).
  • Find a primary clinic or hospital route near where you stay. Paraguay's Ministry of Health publishes a list of public general hospitals. Emergency ambulance SEME is listed as 141 on that page.
  • Meet people through the free ParaguayNext community.

Phone, internet, housing, transport, and health

Phone and internet

  • Buy a local SIM or eSIM when you arrive. Licensed mobile operators appearing in the CONATEL RNST STMC register (05 Mar 2026 PDF) include TELECEL (Tigo), NÚCLEO (Personal), AMX (Claro), and Nubicom.
  • Local prepaid prices change often, so this guide does not list them. Check the operator's own site or shop.
  • Keep your foreign number active during the transition if banks use SMS codes.
  • Home internet options vary by building and city. Install timing depends on the provider and the property. This guide does not publish a national install SLA.

Option: an international eSIM

If your phone supports eSIM, you can buy a travel eSIM in the provider's app before you fly, or when you land. It gives you data right away, before you get a local SIM. These providers list a Paraguay plan. Prices are examples as shown on 2026-11-06 and can change.

  • Airalo: example plan Unlimited data for 3 days, US$19.50. https://www.airalo.com/paraguay-esim
  • Holafly: example plan Unlimited data for 3 days, US$20.90. https://esim.holafly.com/esim-paraguay/
  • Saily: example plan 1 GB for 7 days, US$7.49. https://saily.com/esim-paraguay/
  • Nomad: example plan 1 GB for 7 days, US$6. https://www.nomadesim.com/paraguay-eSIM

Check the provider's page for current prices and coverage before you buy.

Where to live (areas overview)

Neighborhood fit depends on budget, noise, commute, and language. A practical approach is to stay short-term at first and walk candidate areas at different times of day. This guide does not publish rent ranges or "best neighborhood" lists.

Tips:

  • Prefer a short first lease when you can.
  • Check water pressure, generator backup, and who pays utilities.
  • Confirm how you will receive bank cards and letters.

Transport

  • In Asunción, Bolt lists ride service as available. https://bolt.eu/en/cities/asuncion/ Taxis and buses are also common. This draft does not claim Uber availability (Uber Paraguay pages were unreachable on FactCheck day).
  • For Área Metropolitana de Asunción passenger bus fares, Decreto N° 710/2023 Art. 18 sets convencional at Gs. 2.300 and diferencial at Gs. 3.400 per passenger. Confirm no newer decree before you rely on these figures. https://mopc.gov.py/uplmoo9eib8eefou3ooze4y/2024/03/DECRETO-N°710-2023.pdf https://mopc.gov.py/viceministerios/viceministerio-de-transporte/subsidio-transparente/
  • If you will drive, learn the local road rules and get car insurance before you drive. Ask your insurer or a licensed professional in Paraguay what cover you need.

Health care

  • Locate a nearby clinic and pharmacy in your first week.
  • For public general hospitals, see the MSPBS hospital list. https://www.mspbs.gov.py/hospitales-generales.html
  • Understand whether you will use private insurance, pay out of pocket, or another arrangement for day-to-day care. This guide does not list private hospital prices or rankings.
  • Bring a medication list and enough supply for the transition if your doctor agrees.
  • Details on whether any insurance is required for immigration or stay are coming later. For now, check with a licensed professional in Paraguay.

Banking basics for new arrivals

Goal for month one: access cash, receive USD income if you need it, pay local bills in guaraní, and avoid depending on a single card.

This section covers product mechanics and published fees only. It is not tax advice. Details on cross-border tax reporting are coming later. For now, check with a licensed professional in Paraguay.

Layered setup (high level)

  1. International ATM and USD storage (one option many expats discuss): Charles Schwab International, if you qualify.
  2. USD intake and cards (fintech option): Meru, if you qualify and accept fintech tradeoffs.
  3. Local Paraguayan bank: for guaraní bills and local transfers. Sourced examples with live sites on FactCheck day: Itaú Paraguay and Banco Nacional de Fomento (BNF). https://itau.com.py/ https://bnf.gov.py/ Visión Banco is not featured here while its official site remains unreachable.
  4. Crypto bridge tools: treat as optional and high risk. UZPay stays quarantined: the official site returned HTTP 500 on HTTP and TLS failure on HTTPS on 2026-11-06. Do not soften that warning. Do not fund tools you cannot verify live.

This is not a recommendation to open any specific account. Eligibility, fees, and product details change. Confirm on each provider's live site.

Charles Schwab International (sourced summary)

  • Schwab One International is marketed as a U.S. brokerage account for many residents outside the U.S. https://international.schwab.com/us-investing-brokerage-account
  • Online listed U.S. stocks and ETFs are marketed at US$0 commission. Bonds and many mutual funds are not always $0. Check current pricing. https://international.schwab.com/pricing
  • A linked Schwab Bank High Yield Investor Checking account can include a Schwab Bank Visa Platinum Debit Card. https://www.schwab.com/node/30716 https://www.schwab.com/checking/debit-card
  • Schwab Bank refunds ATM fees for cash withdrawals with that debit card at the end of the same month. https://www.schwab.com/checking/faqs
  • Schwab Bank does not assess its own foreign transaction fee on the debit card. Visa network rates still apply. https://www.schwab.com/checking/faqs Schwab Bank Deposit Account Pricing Guide Jan 2026
  • Always decline Dynamic Currency Conversion (DCC) at ATMs. DCC markups are not treated as ATM fees and are not rebated. https://www.schwab.com/checking/faqs https://www.schwab.com/checking/debit-card
  • Paraguay was listed among selectable residences on Schwab's international open-account flow as of 2026-11-06. Confirm before you apply. https://international.schwab.com/open-account-intro
  • Non-U.S. persons generally provide the tax forms and ID materials Schwab lists for international accounts. Details on which Paraguay tax IDs banks accept are coming later. For now, check with a licensed professional in Paraguay, and confirm with Schwab during onboarding. https://international.schwab.com/us-investing-brokerage-account
  • International brokerage marketing describes international wire funding with no Schwab transaction fee on that path. Schwab Bank deposit pricing (Jan 2026) states incoming wires are domestic only for bank deposit accounts. Know which rail you use. https://international.schwab.com/us-investing-brokerage-account https://disclosures.schwab.com/SchwabDashboard/62667/REG30608.pdf
  • Schwab is not a Paraguayan bill-pay rail.

Do not use a Gs. 25,000-40,000 ATM operator fee range. No bank tariff or BCP primary for foreign-card operator fees in that band was found in FactCheck (2026-11-06). This guide publishes no ATM operator fee range.

Meru (sourced summary)

  • Meru markets a U.S. routing and account number for ACH and wire receipts, plus a European IBAN for SEPA. https://getmeru.com/
  • Cards: marketing fetched on check day showed Visa physical and virtual cards. Do not claim Mastercard. https://getmeru.com/ https://getmeru.com/fees/
  • Published personal fees list ACH deposit (Global / Mexico / Colombia) at US$2 and Global wire at US$15. Page note: last update August 2026. Recheck live fees before you budget. https://getmeru.com/fees/
  • ACH timing: Meru Help states credits often take 1 to 3 business days depending on transfer origin. https://getmeru.com/help/es/articles/14488320-para-que-sirve-la-cuenta-bancaria-en-estados-unidos-que-ofrece-meru
  • No mandatory minimum balance to open or keep the account active. https://getmeru.com/help/es/articles/14486446-hay-un-monto-minimo-para-empezar-a-usar-mi-cuenta-meru https://getmeru.com/help/es/articles/14486863-existe-un-saldo-minimo-para-que-mi-cuenta-meru-siga-activa
  • Meru Help lists U.S. bank-account product unavailability for residents of Venezuela, Nicaragua, Cuba, and El Salvador. Paraguay is not on that exclusion list. Confirm in-app for your case. https://getmeru.com/help/es/articles/14489334-que-paises-no-pueden-solicitar-la-cuenta-bancaria-de-ee-uu-en-meru
  • Meru also publishes Paraguay deposit cashback T&Cs for users who reside and have their account registered in Paraguay. https://getmeru.com/help/es/articles/17558394-terminos-y-condiciones-cashback-depositos-paraguay
  • Meru markets platform payouts such as Upwork, Stripe, PayPal, and YouTube. https://getmeru.com/
  • Meru terms identify the company as R3MIT SOLUTIONS INC., registered as a Money Services Business (MSB) with FinCEN, with services via partners. It is not presenting itself as a bank charter. https://getmeru.com/terms-and-conditions/
  • Meru-to-Meru transfers: the Meru Play Store listing says you can send between Meru users with "no hidden fees." Prefer that wording. Do not call Meru-to-Meru "free" unless https://getmeru.com/fees/ later shows an explicit $0 line. Meru on Google Play
  • MoneyGram cash-outs are marketed as globally available subject to local service availability. Paraguay is not named in the Help snippet FactCheck quoted. Confirm locally before you rely on it. https://getmeru.com/help/es/articles/8673980-como-funciona-moneygram
  • Opening fee: Meru Help mentions a US$2 opening cost in the bank-account request flow in some cases (refund language if opening fails after three attempts). The fees page says account opening is free. Both company pages were live on check day. This draft keeps both statements and does not pick a winner. Confirm in-app which applies to you. https://getmeru.com/help/es/articles/8207556-como-solicito-las-cuentas-bancarias https://getmeru.com/fees/

UZPay (quarantined)

  • Official site uzpay.io returned HTTP 500 on HTTP and TLS failure on HTTPS on 2026-11-06. Keep this tool quarantined until the site serves readable terms and fees again.
  • RDAP showed domain registration still active (expiration 2027-01-19; last change 2026-08-12). A Google Play listing for app.ungr.uzpay (UNISOFT LIMITED) was still reachable. That does not mean the website works or that fees are verified. UZPay on Google Play
  • Do not use UZPay as a primary money rail.

Practical month-one money habits

  • Keep at least two working cards from different providers.
  • Decline DCC at ATMs. https://www.schwab.com/checking/faqs
  • Use a local bank for utilities and guaraní transfers when you can.
  • Do not keep all funds in one fintech app.

Meeting people

Arrival is easier when you have humans to ask.

  • Introduce yourself in the New arrivals section of the free ParaguayNext community.
  • Check Events in the community for calls and events when they are posted.
  • Language exchanges, coworking spaces, and hobby groups can help too. Availability varies by city. This guide does not publish a directory.

Be kind, verify advice that affects money or legal status, and never send funds to someone who pressures you.
